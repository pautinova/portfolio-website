BC Ministry of Agriculture

A complete overview of the communications projects I led and supported during my eight-month co-op — spanning email marketing, social media, content writing, event coordination, and website management.

ROLE Communications Co-op
TIMELINE January – August 2025
TEAM Agriservice

Social media

25% INCREASE IN FACEBOOK ENGAGEMENT
TOOLS Figma, Adobe Illustrator

I managed the Ministry's Facebook page, designing social graphics within strict government style guidelines. I built a structured content calendar and increased posting frequency from twice a week to daily.

Email newsletters

48% INCREASE IN SUBSCRIBER LIST (1,800 TO 2,668)
TOOLS Campaign Monitor, later Mailchimp

I created and distributed over 80 newsletters across nine provincial regions and supported the team's migration from Campaign Monitor to Mailchimp. I also rebuilt templates and used engagement data to improve content hierarchy, including moving event information higher and adding a hyperlinked table of contents to make longer newsletters easier to navigate.

Cover crop cards

SEE ALL CARDS →
TOOLS Figma

I also produced over 90 Cover Crop ID cards — quick visual references to help farmers and growers identify species in their early growth stages. This involved summarizing technical content, writing copy, and standardizing layouts for print production.

Factsheets & infographics

TOOLS Figma, Word

Working with agrologists and environmental specialists, I drafted, edited, and designed program overviews, fact sheets, and reports.

Reflection

Thanks for reading to the end!

This co-op marked the start of my career in marketing and communications. Working in a deadline-driven environment taught me how to prioritize competing projects, manage multiple stakeholders, and produce high-quality work under strict guidelines. It also left me eager to work in more experimental, fast-paced environments where ideas are tested, measured, and continuously refined through analytics and experimentation.
