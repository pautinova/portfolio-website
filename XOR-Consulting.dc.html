XOR Consulting

XOR Consulting had evolved beyond its original focus on supply chain consulting, but its website no longer reflected the company's expertise. My role was to redesign the website, reposition the brand, and rewrite the site's content to better communicate its expanded service offerings.

The original website was written when the business focused primarily on supply chain consulting. As the company expanded into data strategy, business intelligence, ERP implementation, and automation, the messaging became outdated and no longer reflected its capabilities.

ROLE Marketing Assistant
TIMELINE July 2026 – Present

Project goals

Reposition the brand

  • Reflect XOR Consulting's expanded expertise in data strategy, automation, ERP implementation, and business intelligence.
  • Replace outdated messaging centered on supply chain consulting.

Modernize the website

  • Improve content hierarchy to make information easier to navigate.
  • Preserve the existing visual identity, themes, colour palette, and brand assets.
  • Design new illustrations that match the website's existing style.

This project is currently in progress.
