KnitPetal

For my AI Today and Tomorrow course, I vibe-coded my first website: a knitting machine calculator that takes a user’s body measurements and automatically generates a customized knitting pattern tailored to their proportions.

I created the knitting calculator because many machine-knitting patterns are based on standard sizes and require manual adjustments. I wanted to automate those calculations.

ROLE Designer & Developer
TIMELINE January – April 2026
TOOLS Claude Code

Project goals

Automate the calculations

  • Remove the manual adjustments machine-knitting patterns usually require.
  • Generate a full pattern from a set of measurements, downloadable as a PDF.

Fit real proportions

  • Tailor patterns to a knitter's own body measurements rather than standard sizes.
  • Let the user choose between two versions: long sleeve and short sleeve.
